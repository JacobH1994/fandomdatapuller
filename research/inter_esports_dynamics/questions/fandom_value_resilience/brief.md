# Does Accumulated Fandom Value Increase Resilience?

Part of `inter_esports_dynamics` — see `../../brief.md` §3 (taxonomy
bucket 2: fandom durability/resilience). Formerly labeled H2, named but
deliberately not tested when first written. Still true here — this is a
genuinely thin, early-stage brief, not a polished companion to
`competitive_exclusion_in_niches/`.

## 1. Hypothesis

Longer-tenured fanbases should be more resilient to shocks (a bad
season, scandal, a flashy new competitor) than newer ones, because
accumulated shared history raises the payoff of continued engagement —
a close parallel to Iannaccone's "religious human capital," where
accumulated ritual and history knowledge raises the expected benefit of
continued participation in a tradition.

## 2. Test (not yet run)

Compare relative viewership drawdown of older titles (League of
Legends, Counter-Strike) during their own past rough patches against
newer titles under comparable stress. Needs multi-year, per-title
volatility data not yet in hand.
